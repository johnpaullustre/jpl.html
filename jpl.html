01 / ABOUT ME

Proposal
Three Promises, One Sincere Heart

I'm John Paul Lustre, and I'm here to show you how much you mean to me through the little things I do. I may not be able to give you everything in the world, but I can offer you my time, effort, honesty, and genuine affection. I can be someone who listens to your stories, supports your dreams, celebrates your happiness, and stays by your side through difficult days. I'm willing to learn, grow, and become a better person—not just for myself, but also for someone as special as you. I won't rush your heart; I'll patiently prove my intentions through sincere actions, one day at a time

01

A Promise to Be Sincere

I promise to be sincere with you in everything I do. I may not be the perfect person, but I will always do my best to show you that my intentions are genuine. I promise to respect your feelings, listen to your thoughts, and appreciate even the smallest moments we share. I won't force you to feel the same way I do, because I believe that genuine feelings take time to grow. I'll be patient and let my actions speak louder than my words. No matter how long it takes, I want you to know that my intentions toward you are honest and true.

02

A Promise to Support You

I promise to support you in pursuing your dreams and becoming the person you want to be. I'll be proud of your achievements, encourage you when things get difficult, and remind you of your worth whenever you doubt yourself. I want to be someone you can comfortably talk to, someone who listens without judging and understands without making you feel small. I cannot promise that every day will be perfect, but I can promise to treat you with kindness, respect, and understanding. Your happiness matters to me, and I hope I can be one of the reasons you smile every day.

03

A Promise to Be Patient and Prove My Love

I promise not to rush things or pressure you into making decisions about your feelings. I understand that winning someone's heart is not about saying the sweetest words, but about showing genuine care through consistent actions. I'll take the time to know you better, appreciate your personality, respect your boundaries, and understand the things that make you happy. I don't expect you to give me an answer right away. Instead, I want to make every moment meaningful and let you see the sincerity behind my intentions. Whatever happens, I hope you will remember that someone genuinely wants to see you happy and is willing to make an honest effort to earn your trust.
